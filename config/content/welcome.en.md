## What this service is for

Prosty Geoportal shows the same public data as the national [geoportal.gov.pl](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl), in a form friendly to people who do not work with maps for a living. Find and browse official data faster and more easily.

You will see the boundaries and number of a parcel, its use in the local or general plan, transaction prices nearby, utility networks and aerial photos. No login and no fees, on a computer and on a phone.

## Where the data comes from

All data comes from the public services of the Head Office of Geodesy and Cartography ([geoportal.gov.pl](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl)) and from the servers of counties, municipalities and other institutions those services connect to. The map loads them as you look and changes or stores nothing. How current the data is depends on the office that keeps it.

> ## Important
>
> The information is for orientation only and is not an official document. Binding data is issued by the county office (land and building register) or the municipality (development plans). Prosty Geoportal is an independent open source service, not a service of GUGiK.
