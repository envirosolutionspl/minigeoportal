EnviroSolutions is committed to making MiniGeoportal (minigeoportal.pl) accessible in line with WCAG 2.1 at level AA (standard EN 301 549), as required by the Polish acts on the digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications and on the accessibility of certain products and services.

### Compliance status

The service is partially compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA because of the non-compliances and exemptions listed below.

- The images of the map, the orthophoto map and the layers come from the services of GUGiK, counties and municipalities and have no text description. The content of the map at a place can be read as text: Enter on the map or a click opens the panel with information about the place, and the search boxes give addresses and parcels as text.
- Layer legends are images supplied by the source services.
- Drawing a distance or area measurement needs a mouse or touch.
- The parcel report opens on geoportal.gov.pl, whose accessibility is the responsibility of GUGiK.

### Keyboard use

- Tab moves between elements; the first ones lead straight to the map or the search.
- On the map: arrow keys move it, plus and minus change the scale, Enter shows information about the middle of the map.
- In the search: arrow keys choose a result, Enter shows it on the map, Esc closes the list.
- Esc closes windows, the place panel and the layer list.

### Display aids

The display button next to the search makes the text larger (A, A+, A++) and switches on high contrast (yellow on black); the choice is remembered. The service works with the browser zoomed to 200% and on narrow screens, has a dark mode that follows the system setting and turns off animations when the system asks for reduced motion. Messages about search results, loading data and measurements are read by screen readers.

### Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 2026-10-21 on the basis of a self-assessment by EnviroSolutions, supported by automated tests.
